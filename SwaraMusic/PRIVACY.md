# Swara: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

**Summary:** Swara has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Your music library stays on your phone.

## Data stored on your device
- Downloaded songs, saved in the app's private folder (`Android/data/com.swara.music/files/Music`)
- Playlists you create, references to audio files you import, and your settings (including an optional Jamendo client ID)
- A small cache of images and API responses

All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup.

## Data sent off your device
Swara has no server of its own. To browse, stream and download, it connects directly to:
- **Wikimedia Commons** (`commons.wikimedia.org`, `upload.wikimedia.org`): [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy)
- **Jamendo** (`api.jamendo.com`, only if you add a client ID): [Jamendo privacy policy](https://www.jamendo.com/legal/privacy)

Like any website, these services receive your IP address, the app's user-agent, and the search terms or files you request.

## Files you import
Swara reads only the audio files you select with Android's file picker. It never uploads, copies or shares them.

## Permissions
- Internet and network state: to stream, and to detect when you're offline
- Foreground media playback and wake lock: to keep music playing with the screen off
- Notifications: for the player controls

Swara does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or broad storage access.

## Children
Swara collects no personal data from anyone.
