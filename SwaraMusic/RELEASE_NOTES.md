**Install on your phone:** download `Swara.apk` below on your Android phone (Android 8.0+), open it, and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser when asked.

Swara streams and downloads only music that is legally free: public-domain and freely licensed recordings from Wikimedia Commons (stream + offline download), optional Jamendo streaming with your own API key, and your own audio files. See the in-app "Music sources & licences" screen for details.

The APK is signed with a demo key that is checked into the repo. It is fine for testing, but sign your own builds before distributing them.
